Service

Project verification & reporting

Mapped, dated project evidence showing what work occurred, where it occurred and whether it met the agreed specification.

A project record that shows what happened and under what conditions

The evidence required should be defined before work begins. Four Peaks can then collect the right flight, weather, application, imagery and spatial records as the job is completed.

The final report brings those records together so a client can assess safety, accuracy and completion against the agreed specification.

Evidence scope

What can be documented

The verification standard is matched to the job, client requirements and the way the record will be used.

Operational records

Information about when and how the aerial work was carried out.

  • Flight records
  • Weather records
  • Chemical application records
  • Other agreed operating information

Spatial and visual evidence

A mapped record of the work area and visible site condition.

  • Treatment and flight coverage maps
  • Before and after imagery
  • Progress imagery
  • GIS data and mapped project features

Project reporting

An organised handover for client, compliance and future planning use.

  • Progress reporting
  • Client-ready verification reports
  • Evidence of accuracy and completion
  • Records aligned to the agreed specification

Reporting package

What you receive

Files are dated, organised and presented as one usable project record rather than a collection of disconnected source material.

  • Dated evidence packProject records grouped and labelled for straightforward review.
  • Mapped extentsThe agreed area, completed coverage and relevant spatial features.
  • Supporting recordsImagery and operational information specified for the job.
  • Verification reportA client-ready account aligned with the agreed completion standard.

Set the reporting standard.

Tell us what work needs verifying, the project specification, who will use the report and what evidence must be collected on the day.
