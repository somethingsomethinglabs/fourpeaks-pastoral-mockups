About

Precision. Practicality. Partnership.

Family-owned aerial land management — Australia-wide, based in Omeo, Victorian High Country.

Four Peaks Pastoral is an Australian family-owned business providing integrated aerial operations, precision agriculture and environmental land management to clients throughout Australia.

Built on more than 24 years of practical farming, agronomy and livestock management, we understand that successful land management is about delivering measurable outcomes, protecting investment and supporting long-term productivity.

We combine advanced drone technology, geospatial intelligence and practical agricultural knowledge across the project lifecycle — from mapping and LiDAR through to application, monitoring and verification.

Base and reach

Omeo base. Australia-wide work.

Crews mobilise nationally under consistent procedures. The High Country is where we are from — not the limit of where we fly.

Who we work with

Partners across Australian landscapes

We work with carbon project developers, forestry managers, government agencies, agricultural businesses and private landholders — delivering safe, consistent operations in challenging country.

Every project is supported by operating procedures, experienced personnel and reporting that shows what was flown and when.
