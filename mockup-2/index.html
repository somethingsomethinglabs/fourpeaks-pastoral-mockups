About

Ground experience. Aerial precision.

Four Peaks Pastoral is a family-owned Australian business built on more than 24 years of practical farming, agronomy and livestock management.

We understand land from the paddock and from above, delivering integrated aerial operations, precision agriculture and environmental land management with measurable outcomes for clients nationwide.

How we work

From first conversation to verified outcomes

A clear, practical process, scoped to your property or program and delivered with consistent procedures and measurable results.

  1. 01

    Scope & advise

    Understand the property, program constraints and intended outcomes, then advise on the right aerial approach, timing and deliverables.

  2. 02

    Plan & prepare

    Flight planning, product and method selection, safety procedures and mobilisation of trained crews and mobile support equipment.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Aerial application, mapping or LiDAR delivered in the field, with nationally consistent operating procedures across varied terrain and regions.

  4. 04

    Report & verify

    Documentation, imagery and data returned in a usable form, so you can verify outcomes and plan the next stage with confidence.

Flagship program

Carbon Grazing Australia™
