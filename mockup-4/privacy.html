Privacy Policy

How Four Peaks Pastoral handles personal information submitted through this website.

Preview stub — final policy text confirmed at launch.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we may collect your name, organisation, phone number, email address, property or project location, service interest, project details, and preferred contact method.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, scope work, and follow up about services relevant to your project. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and access

Enquiry information is received and stored using the website platform and associated email systems used to operate this site. Access is limited to people who need it to respond to enquiries.

Your choices

You can request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you by contacting Four Peaks Pastoral using the details on the Contact page.
