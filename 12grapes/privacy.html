Privacy and collection notice

Website preview. Forms on this preview do not transmit or store information. Business details and a live data-handling process must be confirmed before online submission is enabled.

Current preview behaviour

No form endpoint, client recipient, candidate recipient or verified contact details were supplied with this build. In its current configuration, the website does not transmit form information. It prepares a local text brief that the visitor can copy or download.

If an email address is added in site-config.js, the website opens the visitor's email application with the details filled in. The visitor must review and send that email themselves. If a form endpoint is added, the website shows a received message only after the endpoint returns a successful HTTP response.

Client enquiries

The client form asks for information used to assess and respond to a vineyard services enquiry:

  • name and optional business or vineyard name;
  • phone and email;
  • vineyard location or postcode;
  • service needed, timing and approximate scope;
  • other job details supplied by the visitor.

The final collection notice should identify the legal entity collecting this information, who receives it, how it is stored, whether it is disclosed to service providers, and how a person can request access or correction.

Candidate enquiries

The candidate expression-of-interest form is separate because recruitment information has a different purpose and retention risk. It may include location, availability, work rights, transport, experience, preferred work and licences or tickets.

Before the candidate form goes live, confirm who can access the information, how suitability is assessed, how long unsuccessful expressions of interest are retained, and how deletion requests are handled.

Before online submission is enabled

Confirm and add:

  • the legal business name and privacy contact;
  • the applicable privacy obligations and approved collection wording;
  • the form service, email system or CRM receiving each enquiry type;
  • storage location, access controls, disclosures and retention periods;
  • the process for access, correction, complaints and deletion;
  • any analytics, cookies or third-party services introduced before launch.

Return to client enquiries or candidate enquiries.
