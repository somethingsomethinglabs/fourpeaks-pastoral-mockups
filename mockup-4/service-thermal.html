Service

Thermal wildlife & animal surveys

Thermal and RGB aerial surveys for locating livestock, pest animals and wildlife across large or difficult sites.

Detect animals when colour imagery is not enough

Thermal sensors detect heat signatures that may be difficult to see in daylight imagery. Survey timing, weather, vegetation and flight height are planned around the target species and site.

Thermal observations can be paired with RGB imagery and GIS locations, giving the client both a detection record and the visual context around it.

Scope

What the service covers

The survey method and reporting are set by the target, area, site conditions and level of identification required.

Pest and wildlife surveys

Targeted detection and observation over areas that are difficult to cover from the ground.

  • Pest animal detection and population surveys
  • Feral deer, wild dog, fox and pig monitoring
  • Kangaroo population assessments
  • Native wildlife and habitat monitoring

Livestock location

Aerial search support across large, vegetated or difficult properties.

  • Livestock location and recovery support
  • Searches across difficult or remote sites
  • Georeferenced observations and waypoints
  • Survey methods that minimise disturbance

Project and environmental work

Thermal evidence for operational planning and ecological programs.

  • Pre-operational environmental assessment
  • Plantation and carbon project monitoring
  • Biodiversity reporting support
  • Combined thermal, RGB and GIS outputs

Survey record

What you receive

Results are supplied with the location and survey context needed to interpret what was observed.

  • Georeferenced observationsMapped detections or waypoints across the survey area.
  • Survey notesCounts, conditions, method and relevant limits on interpretation.
  • Thermal and RGB imageryEvidence and surrounding visual context where included in the scope.
  • GIS reportAgreed spatial files and client-ready reporting for management or project use.

Scope the thermal survey.

Tell us the target species, survey area, vegetation, preferred timing and the level of identification or reporting required.
