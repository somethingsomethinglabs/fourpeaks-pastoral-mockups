Program

Carbon Grazing Australia™

A method for integrating livestock into environmental planting and carbon project work — with aerial mapping and verification built into the process.

The method

What the program sets

Grazing timing, stocking and rest are set against planting and monitoring milestones. The aerial record sits alongside the grazing plan — not as a separate story.

  • Baseline Map the block before livestock and planting decisions lock in — orthomosaic and, where needed, terrain products.
  • Grazing design Define when stock enter and leave relative to planting, weed pressure and seasonal feed.
  • Progress flights Capture dated imagery at agreed milestones so change on country is visible.
  • Verification pack Hand over treatment maps, flight logs and imagery in a file list you can file.

Deliverables

What you receive

Process artefacts — not outcome claims. Carbon Grazing Australia™ describes how grazing and aerial verification are sequenced; registry or credit outcomes sit with the project’s own methodology and auditors.

  • Baseline map package
  • Grazing / rest milestone schedule
  • Progress imagery set (dated)
  • Verification file list and GIS layers
  • Treatment or monitoring notes where flown

Who it is for

Landholders and project managers

Designed for people who need livestock and planting to coexist on the same country — with a clear aerial record for each stage.
