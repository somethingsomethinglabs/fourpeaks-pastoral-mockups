Service

Precision aerial application

Safe, accurate application of herbicides, fertilisers, seed and agricultural products across agricultural, environmental and carbon projects throughout Australia.

Capability for terrain where ground rigs struggle

Four Peaks Pastoral delivers precision aerial application services using industry-leading drone technology to safely and accurately apply herbicides, fertilisers, seed and other agricultural products across agricultural, environmental and carbon projects throughout Australia.

Our specialised drone fleet enables us to operate efficiently across all terrain types, from broadacre paddocks and rolling grazing country through to steep hillsides, rugged forestry, revegetation sites and remote landscapes where conventional machinery is unable to operate safely or economically.

Capability

  • Pre-plant site preparation
  • Precision herbicide application
  • Grass overspray programs
  • Woody weed and blackberry control
  • Broadleaf weed management
  • Spot spraying and targeted weed control
  • Revegetation and environmental planting support
  • Precision fertiliser application
  • Seed spreading and aerial seeding
  • Remote and difficult terrain operations
  • Plantation establishment support
  • Post-treatment monitoring and verification

Deliverables

Typical outputs

  • Flight logs
  • Treatment map
  • Product record
