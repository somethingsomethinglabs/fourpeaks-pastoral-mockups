Environmental & carbon project support

Integrated aerial operations and technical support from planning through monitoring for restoration, revegetation and carbon sequestration projects.

Support across the full project lifecycle

Environmental and carbon projects need consistent aerial support from baseline through establishment and ongoing monitoring.

We integrate mapping, application and verification so project managers receive stage packs they can use for planning, compliance and reporting.

Capability

  • High-resolution baseline aerial mapping
  • LiDAR terrain and vegetation surveys
  • Project planning and geospatial analysis
  • Pre-plant site preparation
  • Precision herbicide application
  • Weed and pest vegetation management
  • Plantation establishment support
  • Vegetation health monitoring
  • Tree survival and establishment assessments
  • Fuel load and dry matter monitoring

Deliverables

Typical outputs

  • Baseline maps
  • Progress imagery
  • Stage packs
