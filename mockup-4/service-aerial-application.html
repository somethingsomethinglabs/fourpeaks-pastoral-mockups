Service

Precision aerial application

Targeted aerial application for country, vegetation and project sites that need accurate placement without relying on ground access.

Application planned around the site

The job starts with the target area, terrain, vegetation, access, product, timing and result required. Four Peaks then builds an operating plan for the site rather than treating it as a standard flight.

Specialised drone systems allow work on broadacre paddocks, grazing country, steep slopes, forestry, revegetation sites and remote areas where ground equipment may be unsafe or impractical.

Scope

What the service covers

Application can sit on its own or form part of a wider mapping, establishment, monitoring and verification program.

Site preparation and weed control

Targeted work before planting or during ongoing land management.

  • Pre-plant site preparation
  • Precision herbicide application
  • Grass overspray programs
  • Woody weed, blackberry and broadleaf control
  • Spot spraying and targeted treatment

Establishment and inputs

Application support for productive and environmental planting programs.

  • Revegetation and environmental planting support
  • Precision fertiliser application
  • Seed spreading and aerial seeding
  • Plantation establishment support

Access, control and follow-up

Flight planning and records suited to difficult sites and accountable programs.

  • Remote and difficult terrain operations
  • RTK positioning and terrain-following systems
  • Calibration and weather monitoring
  • Post-treatment monitoring and verification

Project record

What you receive

The reporting package is agreed with the job, so the client receives a usable record of the work rather than a pile of flight files.

  • Treatment mapThe agreed work area and completed flight coverage.
  • Operating recordsFlight, weather and product information relevant to the application.
  • Completion evidenceDated records suitable for project files and client reporting.
  • Follow-up evidencePost-treatment imagery or verification where it forms part of the scope.

Tell us what needs treating, where the site is, the timing, access constraints and what record you need at completion.
