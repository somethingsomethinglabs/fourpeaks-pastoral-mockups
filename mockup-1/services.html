Services

Survey. Treat. Verify.

Seven capabilities across the job lifecycle — Australia-wide, based in Omeo, Victorian High Country. Open any service for deliverables, quoting and limits.

Most people come to us to spray country a ground rig can’t reach, to map a property before a carbon project, or to prove work was done.

Survey & measure

  1. 01

    Aerial mapping & surveying

    RTK survey-grade imagery for planning and monitoring.

    Orthomosaic · DSM/DTM · typically 7–10 days
  2. 02

    LiDAR & terrain intelligence

    Terrain and canopy structure through vegetation.

    Point cloud · Contours · by density
  3. 03

    Agricultural intelligence

    Pasture and crop layers for seasonal decisions.

    Index layers · Season note
  4. 04

    Thermal wildlife & animal surveys

    Locate stock, pests and wildlife with low disturbance.

    Thermal imagery · Count notes

Treat & apply

  1. 05

    Precision aerial application

    Herbicides, fertilisers, seed across all terrain.

    Treatment map · Flight log · ~5 days
  2. 06

    Environmental & carbon project support

    Restoration and carbon lifecycle aerial support.

    Progress imagery · Stage packs

Record & verify

  1. 07

    Project verification & reporting

    Dated evidence packs for compliance and planning.

    Evidence pack · File list

Quoting

How quoting works

We quote per hectare or area once we have seen the country, the task and the deliverables you need. Mobilisation distance and terrain change the number — we say so before you commit.

Coverage is Australia-wide. Crews mobilise from our Omeo High Country base and operate under nationally consistent procedures.
