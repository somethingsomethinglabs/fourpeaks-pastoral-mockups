Service

LiDAR survey & terrain intelligence

Three-dimensional terrain and vegetation data for sites where canopy or complex landform limits what conventional photography can show.

See the ground and vegetation structure in the same survey

LiDAR records millions of three-dimensional points from the ground, vegetation and built features. Those points can be classified and turned into terrain, surface and canopy information.

The survey can also be integrated with RGB imagery, RTK positioning, multispectral data and existing GIS layers to give the project one spatial reference.

Scope

What the service covers

The required point density, accuracy, classification and derived outputs are set by the project use.

Terrain and water

Ground-level information for planning across uneven or vegetated sites.

  • Digital Terrain and Surface Models
  • Contours, terrain and slope analysis
  • Drainage and water flow assessment
  • Erosion assessment

Vegetation and carbon

Three-dimensional information about vegetation height, cover and structure.

  • Vegetation structure and canopy analysis
  • Forest inventory and biomass assessment
  • Environmental and carbon project planning
  • Plantation design and establishment

Property and infrastructure

Measured terrain and features for development, access and asset decisions.

  • Earthworks and volumetric calculations
  • Asset and infrastructure surveys
  • Property and farm planning
  • GIS integration and derived layers

Survey handover

What you receive

The raw survey is processed into the point cloud, models and working layers agreed at the start of the project.

  • Point cloudGeoreferenced and classified three-dimensional survey data.
  • Terrain and surface modelsGround and above-ground surfaces prepared for the agreed use.
  • Derived layersContours, drainage, canopy, slope or volume information specified for the project.
  • GIS-ready handoverAgreed spatial files, maps and supporting notes for the project team.
