Service 03

LiDAR survey & terrain intelligence

Three-dimensional terrain and vegetation intelligence for complex sites and decisions.

What we do

Built for real operating conditions

LiDAR captures accurate three-dimensional data across complex landscapes. It can measure vegetation structure and identify ground surfaces beneath canopy where conventional aerial imagery is limited.

The resulting terrain, surface and vegetation models support environmental work, plantation design, earthworks, water planning, biomass assessment and property-scale decision making.

03 / 07

LiDAR capability

  • Environmental and carbon project planning
  • Plantation design and establishment
  • Digital Terrain and Surface Models
  • Contour, terrain and slope analysis
  • Drainage and water flow assessment
  • Earthworks planning and volumetric calculations
  • Vegetation structure and canopy analysis
  • Forest inventory and biomass assessment
  • Erosion assessment
  • Property and farm planning

See the terrain beneath the canopy

LiDAR gives project teams a measured base for design and monitoring. Detailed elevation and vegetation data can reduce site uncertainty before crews, machinery or planting programs are committed.

Where it fits

Common project uses

  1. 01

    Vegetated terrain

    Measure ground levels and terrain form beneath canopy where standard imagery has limits.

  2. 02

    Water and earthworks planning

    Assess slope, drainage, flow paths, erosion and proposed earthworks.

  3. 03

    Canopy and biomass assessment

    Record vegetation height, structure and density for forestry or environmental work.
