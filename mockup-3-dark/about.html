About us

Precision. Practicality. Partnership.

A family-owned team delivering aerial land management across Australia.

Four Peaks Pastoral is a family-owned Australian business providing aerial application, precision agriculture and environmental land management to clients across the country.

Built on more than 24 years of practical farming, agronomy and livestock management, we understand that successful land management is about more than completing a task. It is about protecting your investment, supporting long-term productivity and delivering work you can verify.

Today, Four Peaks combines drone technology, aerial mapping and practical agricultural knowledge to support environmental planting — from high-resolution mapping and LiDAR surveying through to precision herbicide application, plantation establishment, ongoing monitoring and verification.

Who we work with

Partners across Australian landscapes

Our team works alongside carbon project developers, forestry managers, government agencies, agricultural businesses and private landholders to deliver safe, consistent operations in some of Australia’s most challenging country.

Because we've farmed ourselves, we plan aerial work the way a land manager would — experienced crews, sound procedures, and reporting you can actually use to check the work and plan what comes next.

Mission

To improve the way Australia's landscapes are managed by combining practical agricultural experience with innovative aerial technology that delivers measurable environmental and commercial outcomes.

Vision

To become Australia's most trusted integrated aerial and land management partner, setting the benchmark for precision, innovation and practical delivery across agriculture, forestry, environmental restoration and carbon farming.
