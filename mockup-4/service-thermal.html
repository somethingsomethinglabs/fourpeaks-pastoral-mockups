Thermal wildlife & animal surveys

Thermal imaging to locate livestock, pest animals and wildlife across large areas while minimising disturbance.

Find what conventional imagery misses

Thermal surveys locate livestock, pest animals and wildlife across large areas with minimal disturbance — information conventional daylight imagery often misses.

We return thermal mosaics, count notes and waypoints you can use for management, recovery and ecological reporting.

  • Feral deer, wild dog, fox and pig monitoring
  • Kangaroo population assessments
  • Livestock location and recovery
  • Native wildlife identification and habitat monitoring
  • Pre-operational environmental assessments
  • Plantation and carbon project wildlife monitoring
  • Biodiversity and ecological reporting support
