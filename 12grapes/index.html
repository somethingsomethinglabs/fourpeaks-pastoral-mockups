Ways to engage

The job structure should match the operating need.

A single seasonal task and a longer development project need different planning. These are useful starting points for the first conversation.

01

One-off seasonal work

A defined task within a particular vineyard or weather window.

02

Scheduled operations

Several planned tasks or repeat visits across a season.

03

Labour and machinery packages

A combined brief where people, supervision and equipment need to work as one scope.

04

Development project support

Sequenced project work that needs access, responsibilities and dependencies set out first.

05

Ongoing support

A recurring arrangement with agreed responsibilities, communication and rate basis.

Capability and mobilisation

People and equipment are useful only when they fit the site.

Equipment, crew size and mobilisation need to be matched to the task, access and operating window. The enquiry captures the details needed to confirm availability, inclusions and constraints before a quote or booking.

PeopleTask, crew requirement, supervision and communication.
EquipmentMachine or implement needs, operator requirements and inclusions.
SiteLocation, access, row conditions and relevant constraints.
TimingOperating window, dependencies and scheduling pressure.

How a job starts

Enough detail to make the next conversation useful.

An initial brief does not need to settle every technical detail. It gets the right information in front of the right person.

01

Send the essentials

Service, location, timing, approximate scope and contact details.

02

Confirm fit

Review the operating need, site access, timing and any obvious constraints.

03

Agree the basis

Set out the scope, inclusions, rate or proposal basis and mobilisation requirements.

04

Plan delivery

Once agreed, confirm people, equipment, timing, communication and close-out arrangements.

Rates and availability

Ask for the current 2026 charge-out information against a real scope.

A useful rate conversation should cover the service, location and timing, plus GST, minimum hours, travel or mobilisation, operator and fuel inclusions, and any seasonal conditions that apply.
