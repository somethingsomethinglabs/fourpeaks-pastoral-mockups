Service

Aerial mapping & surveying

High-resolution aerial data collected for the decision you need to make, from property planning and earthworks to environmental monitoring.

A survey built around the question

The scope starts with what needs to be measured, monitored or planned. Capture method, ground detail, accuracy and file formats are then matched to that use.

RGB and RTK mapping can be combined with LiDAR, multispectral, thermal or existing GIS data when the project needs more than a conventional aerial image.

Scope

What the service covers

Each survey is planned around the area, required accuracy, site conditions and the way the final data will be used.

Core aerial mapping

Current, high-detail imagery tied to a reliable spatial reference.

  • High-resolution RGB orthomosaics
  • RTK survey-grade capture
  • Property and boundary mapping
  • Asset and infrastructure mapping

Terrain and measurement

Models and measurements for landform, design and quantity decisions.

  • Digital Surface and Terrain Models
  • Contours, elevation and slope analysis
  • Volumetric surveys
  • Construction and earthworks mapping

Monitoring and GIS

Repeatable spatial data for projects that continue beyond one flight.

  • Environmental and plantation mapping
  • Progress and compliance monitoring
  • GIS data collection and management
  • Integration with other sensor data

Survey handover

What you receive

Outputs are supplied in practical formats for viewing, measuring, planning or adding to an existing spatial workflow.

  • OrthomosaicA georeferenced image of the full survey area at the agreed resolution.
  • Terrain modelsSurface, ground, contour or slope products where required.
  • Measured layersBoundaries, assets, quantities or project features defined in the scope.
  • GIS handoverAgreed files and metadata ready for the client's mapping environment.
