Our services

Integrated aerial operations & land management

Geospatial intelligence and practical solutions that support agricultural enterprises, environmental restoration, carbon projects, forestry operations and government agencies throughout Australia.

By combining more than 24 years of practical agricultural experience with industry-leading drone technology, precision aerial application systems and advanced geospatial intelligence, we transform complex land management challenges into informed, data-driven decisions.

01: Precision aerial application

Precision aerial application

Four Peaks Pastoral delivers precision aerial application services using industry-leading drone technology to safely and accurately apply herbicides, fertilisers, seed and other agricultural products across agricultural, environmental and carbon projects throughout Australia.

Our specialised drone fleet enables us to operate efficiently across all terrain types, from broadacre paddocks and rolling grazing country through to steep hillsides, rugged forestry, revegetation sites and remote landscapes where conventional machinery is unable to operate safely or economically.

  • Pre-plant site preparation
  • Precision herbicide application
  • Grass overspray programs
  • Woody weed and blackberry control
  • Broadleaf weed management
  • Spot spraying and targeted weed control
  • Revegetation and environmental planting support
  • Precision fertiliser application
  • Seed spreading and aerial seeding
  • Remote and difficult terrain operations
  • Plantation establishment support
  • Post-treatment monitoring and verification

Aerial mapping & surveying

High-resolution aerial mapping and survey services that provide accurate, reliable and actionable information for agricultural, environmental and infrastructure projects across Australia, using advanced RTK-enabled drone technology for survey-grade imagery and geospatial data.

  • Contour mapping

  • Progress and compliance monitoring
  • GIS data collection and management

03: LiDAR survey & terrain intelligence

LiDAR survey & terrain intelligence

LiDAR enables Four Peaks to capture highly accurate three-dimensional data across complex landscapes. Unlike conventional aerial photography, LiDAR can penetrate vegetation canopies to generate detailed terrain models, measure vegetation structure and accurately map ground surfaces.

  • Environmental and carbon project planning
  • Plantation design and establishment
  • Digital Terrain and Surface Models
  • Contour, terrain and slope analysis
  • Drainage and water flow assessment
  • Earthworks planning and volumetric calculations
  • Vegetation structure and canopy analysis
  • Forest inventory and biomass assessment
  • Erosion assessment
  • Property and farm planning

06: Thermal wildlife & animal surveys

Thermal wildlife & animal surveys

Thermal imaging detects and locates animals that are often difficult or impossible to identify using conventional aerial imagery, surveying large areas efficiently while minimising disturbance to livestock and native wildlife.

  • Feral deer, wild dog, fox and pig monitoring
  • Kangaroo population assessments
  • Livestock location and recovery
  • Native wildlife identification and habitat monitoring
  • Pre-operational environmental assessments
  • Plantation and carbon project wildlife monitoring
  • Biodiversity and ecological reporting support
