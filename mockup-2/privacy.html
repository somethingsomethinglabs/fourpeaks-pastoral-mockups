Privacy Policy

How Four Peaks Pastoral handles personal information submitted through this website.

Preview policy. Final wording and contact details will be confirmed before launch.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we may collect your name, business or organisation, phone number, email address, property or project location, service interest and any project details you provide.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, scope work and follow up about services relevant to your project. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and access

Enquiry information is received and stored using the website platform and associated email systems used to operate this site. Access is limited to people who need it to respond to enquiries or administer those systems.

Website analytics

The website may collect basic usage information such as page views, device type and referring page. Analytics must not record enquiry form contents.

Your choices

You can request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you by contacting Four Peaks Pastoral.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the Contact page.
