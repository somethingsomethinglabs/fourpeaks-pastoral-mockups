Service

Agricultural intelligence

Practical aerial information for crop and pasture monitoring, farm assets, land condition and property development planning.

Information interpreted in the context of farm work

A useful farm survey needs more than a technically correct image. It needs to reflect the season, ground conditions, management question and the way the property operates.

Four Peaks combines aerial data with more than 24 years of practical agricultural experience to produce information that can support a real management decision.

Scope

What the service covers

Surveys can answer one immediate question or establish a repeatable view of seasonal and property change.

Crop and pasture

Repeatable information about establishment, condition and production.

  • Crop health and establishment assessment
  • Pasture condition and productivity
  • Biomass and dry matter estimation
  • Seasonal monitoring and weed identification

Assets and property

A current view of farm infrastructure and how the property is laid out.

  • Livestock water infrastructure mapping
  • Fence line and asset inspections
  • Paddock and property planning
  • Property development planning

Land and farm data

Spatial layers for managing land condition and keeping farm records useful.

  • Soil erosion and drainage assessment
  • Precision agriculture support
  • Farm mapping and GIS management
  • Integration with existing farm data

Farm-ready output

What you receive

The output is shaped around the management question, with enough context to make the imagery and data useful after the flight.

  • Annotated imageryCurrent aerial views with agreed areas, features or observations identified.
  • Seasonal comparisonComparable capture and notes where monitoring is repeated over time.
  • Asset and condition layersMapped infrastructure, land condition or production information.
  • Farm GISAgreed spatial files that can remain part of the property's working record.
