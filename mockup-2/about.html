About us

Precision. Practicality. Partnership.

Delivering integrated land management solutions across Australia.

Four Peaks Pastoral is an Australian family-owned business providing integrated aerial operations, precision agriculture and environmental land management services to clients throughout Australia.

Built on more than 24 years of practical farming, agronomy and livestock management experience, we understand that successful land management is about much more than completing a task. It is about delivering measurable outcomes, protecting investment and supporting long-term productivity.

Today, Four Peaks combines advanced drone technology, geospatial intelligence and practical agricultural knowledge to provide complete solutions across the environmental planting lifecycle, from high-resolution mapping and LiDAR surveying through to precision herbicide application, plantation establishment support, ongoing monitoring and verification.

Who we work with

Partners across Australian landscapes

Our team works alongside carbon project developers, forestry managers, government agencies, agricultural businesses and private landholders to deliver safe, consistent and scalable operations in some of Australia’s most challenging environments.

What sets Four Peaks apart is our ability to integrate technology with practical on-ground experience. Every project is supported by robust operating procedures, experienced personnel and comprehensive reporting that provides our clients with complete confidence in the work delivered.

Our vision

Australia’s leading integrated aerial operations partner

We continue to invest in aircraft, remote sensing, AI and specialised equipment, while staying focused on one aim: professional aerial solutions for agriculture, environmental restoration and carbon landscape management, whether on a single property or across national programs.
